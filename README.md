# From Stimulus to Response, and Back Again: The Cycle of Adaptive Progress

> "When playing a game of cards, it's wise to observe carefully as you draw a new card, weigh your options, and adapt your strategy if needed."

## **Nexus Series - Paper 02**

This paper mainly focuses on reviewing existing materials on observation-reaction processes, exploring how individuals gather information, perceive, evaluate, and adapt to developing circumstances.

We hypothesise the following model can effectively describe the steps in these processes, from observation to reaction:
- Observation: Gathering raw input.
- Deliberate Reflection: Processing observations.
- Decision-Making: Potential adjustments to a course.
- Action: Taking the next chosen step.
